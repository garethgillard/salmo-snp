Conditions

Tiers count chromatin activity in all 21 conditions: tissues (brain, gonad, liver, muscle; immature or mature, female or male) and the 5 embryo stages. The Table's Chromatin filter then keeps SNPs in a robust ATAC peak (or an active state) in the conditions you pick, without changing any tier. The portal names sample conditions only, never a phenotype.

Chromatin states

AQUA-FAANG's ChromHMM state labels, known only inside robust ATAC peaks. BodyMap (adult tissues) and DevMap (embryo stages) are separate 12-state models. The states have no written definitions yet: see the emission figure (ATAC, H3K27ac, H3K4me1, H3K4me3 and H3K27me3 signal per state).

Fine-mapping of GWAS loci

Loci: SNPs within ±{{ finemap.window|thousands }} bp of each lead with p < {{ finemap.suggestive }}, strongest first, by distance only (no LD). Each locus is fine-mapped assuming one causal variant and no LD: Wakefield approximate Bayes factors with W = {{ finemap.prior_sd }}² on the standardised scale (betas divided by a trait SD of {{ finemap.trait_sd }}; without se, V = 1/(2N·af·(1−af))). The uniform prior gives every SNP the same weight; the functional prior π ∝ exp({{ finemap.functional_lambda }} × tier_score) over all 21 conditions. {% widthratio finemap.coverage 1 100 %}% credible sets in descending PIP.

Alleles are turned to the Ssal_v3.1 reference (alt = effect allele) using the genome, else the 504-fish panel sites; p values are read as text, so p = 1e-400 stays 400 on the −log10 scale.
