AQUA-FAANG samples

The chromatin states, ATAC peaks, signal and molQTL come from the AQUA-FAANG atlases. BodyMap: 12 AquaGen commercial (domesticated diploid) fish, born at the fish research centre of NMBU in Ås and reared in fresh water in a closed recirculating system; immature fish (J) born January 2020, mature fish (A) March 2019, all sampled in September 2020 (BioSamples SAMEA9253105–116). Brain, gonad, liver and muscle (gill RNA only). DevMap: embryo pools at five stages (Roslin Institute). Gill ATAC and CTCF ChIP were discarded; histone ChIP has 2 of 3 replicates except in gonad.
