Quick start

  1. Score a list: a file, pasted positions, genes or regions, structural variants or GWAS results, or try an example. The Score page lists the results this browser opened.
  2. Read the Overview: how many SNPs are in each score band, where they lie in genes, and which are near a known locus.
  3. Filter the Table on its columns, and click a SNP to see its evidence card.
  4. Add the SNPs you want to your Design panel, compare it with your chip and download vendor files.
  5. No list yet? Search for a gene, rs ID or region, or browse the Known loci.

Words used

Score
How much functional evidence a SNP has: the sum of the weights of its evidence, over all 27 conditions, so a SNP has the same score everywhere. You can choose the weights (Weights, in the Table). 0 = no evidence found, which is not evidence of no effect. {% if example %}For example, {{ example }}.{% endif %} All the weights
Score band
Scores are coloured by band:{% for tier, range, note in tiers %} {{ tier|band }}{% endfor %} (the strongest first; your weights never move the bands' edges).
Condition
One AQUA-FAANG sample group: a tissue of fish of one maturity and sex, head kidney after one immune treatment, or one embryo stage. Scores count all 27.
Chromatin state
What the chromatin is doing at a SNP in one condition, such as Active enhancer or Active TSS, known only inside a robust ATAC peak. Active states count the most. Their colours
Known locus
A published SNP, gene region or structural variant from the Known loci catalogue, marked ★. Your SNPs within {{ window|thousands }} bp of one are marked too.
Public SNP
A variant found in 504 public fish, wild and farmed, pooled (the allele frequencies, MAF). Gene pages list the common ones, and the Overview compares your list with them.
Design panel
Your working list of SNPs for a genotyping chip or panel: add SNPs from any result, compare them with your chip and download vendor files. Open it

More terms (MAF, LORe, molQTL, PIP and others) are in the glossary on Methods.

Questions

Why don't scores change when I filter by tissue?

Scores always count all 27 conditions, so a SNP has the same score in the Overview, its card, the Design panel and every download. The Table's Chromatin filter only picks rows: SNPs inside a robust ATAC peak (or, if you choose, an active state) in at least one of the conditions you tick.

What do the chromatin colours mean?

Each colour is one AQUA-FAANG chromatin state, named in the colour key. Methods says what each counts as, next to the score bands and the evidence weights.

Where are my results?

The Score page lists the results this browser opened, newest first, until they are deleted ({% if local %}with Delete: this copy runs on your computer and keeps them until then{% else %}{{ keep_hours }} hours after scoring, or Delete{% endif %}). The list lives in your browser{% if not local %}; anyone with a results link can open those results{% endif %}.

Who can see my Design panel?

Only this browser: a cookie names the panel, never a link. {% if local %}It is kept until you clear it.{% else %}It is deleted {{ keep_hours }} hours after you start it; to keep it longer, download its TSV and score that file again later.{% endif %}

What if my positions are on ICSASG_v2?

The portal needs Ssal_v3.1 positions. Rows with an rs ID in their id column are moved for you: when a row's position is its rs ID's ICSASG_v2 position in EVA release 9, it gets the rs ID's Ssal_v3.1 position (alleles complemented where the strands differ; the downloaded table's position_source column names those rows). Other positions can be converted offline with the conversion script in the salmo-snp repository. When more than {{ assembly_share }} of the SNPs checked against the genome have a reference mismatch or look strand-flipped, the upload check warns that the list is probably on ICSASG_v2 or the other strand.

Which chromosome names work?

1–29, as in Ensembl and AQUA-FAANG, plus the aliases in the release's alias table{% if chrom_aliases %}, for example for chromosome 25: {{ chrom_aliases|join:", " }} (any case){% endif %}. The upload check lists every alias it applied. SNPs on unplaced scaffolds are listed as rejected.

Can I search or score rs IDs and LOC symbols?

Search finds rs IDs from EVA release 9 (rs IDs on several Ssal_v3.1 positions are left out) and opens their variant page, which also gives the ICSASG_v2 position. A file of rs IDs can be scored like any list (format). NCBI LOC symbols are found only for genes whose Ensembl description cites the NCBI gene; otherwise use the gene's name, ortholog or position.

Can I upload a chip manifest as it is?

Yes: Illumina manifests and Axiom annotation files are read as they are. Their positions are often on ICSASG_v2 and their alleles on the TOP/BOT or probe strand, so watch the assembly warning and the Table's Ref. check column.

Which AQUA-FAANG data does the Locus browser show?

Signal (RNA-seq, ATAC-seq and ChIP-seq, the mean of each condition's replicates), chromatin states and robust ATAC peaks, read from salmobase for the region on screen: the BodyMap tissues, the DevMap embryo stages and the ImmunoMap, head kidney after PBS, poly I:C or Vibrio ('HK' rows: in vivo; 'HK cells': leukocytes in vitro, signal only, not counted).

In salmon the ImmunoMap (six fish per treatment; Naseer et al. 2025, Genome Research) has states from its own six-state model, whose three combined labels have their own colours, and gill ATAC-seq was re-made in 2026 (weaker than the other tissues; gill states from their own model); both count in the scores from release 2026-11-20. In trout the ImmunoMap (INRAE) has states for PBS and for Vibrio, from one model each, and none for poly I:C; they count in the scores from release trout-2026-11-25. Hover a row's name in the browser for its details.

What are the predicted allele effects (ChromBPNet)?

A ChromBPNet model learns from one tissue's ATAC-seq reads how DNA sequence shapes open chromatin, and then predicts the accessibility around a SNP twice, once with each allele. The score is the size of the predicted change (the absolute log2 fold change, alt over ref). An arrow in the Table and on the card, and in the Locus browser a tick up in red or down in blue, says whether the alt allele is predicted more or less accessible; 'top 1%' ranks the score among 100,000 random public SNPs.

The first model is gill's, for Atlantic salmon, scored in release 2026-11-25 for the public SNPs inside the model's own peaks: a SNP outside that set shows 'not scored', which is not a low score. Any SNP's card can also run the model for that SNP alone ('Predict with ChromBPNet Gill': a second or two, where this portal runs), and then shows the predicted signal with each allele. These are a model's predictions, not measurements, and they do not count in the scores.

Exports and vendor files

How are SNPs named in vendor files?

Vendor files name each SNP Ssa<chromosome>p<position>, for example {{ locus_name }} (letters and digits only, unique; chromosome 0 = not on 1–29), because your ids may break vendor naming rules. The lookup file maps each name back to your id, position, alleles, TOP/BOT strand, Illumina A/B alleles, whether it needs two probes (A/T or C/G: Infinium I), assay status and export notes.

Why are some SNPs left out of vendor files?

SNPs that fail the assay check, are not checked or have no genome sequence are left out, unless you keep them, flagged, under More formats. A vendor file holds at most {{ vendor_cap|thousands }} SNPs, with flanks from the Ssal_v3.1 genome and alleles on its forward strand. Assay thresholds are provisional: confirm the layout and the designs with your vendor.

Illumina Infinium bins (error 111, warning 604)

A/T SNPs go to Infinium I bin A, C/G SNPs to bin B and the others to Infinium II bin C. A used bin A or B needs at least {{ bins.0 }} SNPs and bin C at least {{ bins.2|thousands }} (error 111); {{ bins.1 }} are recommended in A and B for polyploids such as salmon (warning 604).

SNPs within {{ close_bp }} bp of another (warning 340)

Neighbouring markers can interfere with each other's probes (Illumina warning 340; Thermo Fisher: interfering SNPs can be as close as 10 bases).

What does a download say about how it was made?

Every download says which release, rules and data produced it: '#' lines at the top of TSV and BED files, and a provenance file next to vendor files (the zip bundle holds both).

Deleted
With Delete on a result or a track, or Clear on the Design panel; removing the data folder removes everything. Exported files contain your SNPs, so share them as you would the list.
