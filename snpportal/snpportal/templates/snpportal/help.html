Quick start

  1. Score a file, pasted positions, genes or regions, or try an example. The Score page lists the results this browser opened.
  2. Read the Overview: tiers count all 21 sample conditions.
  3. Filter the Table on its columns (Chromatin: a peak in the tissues you pick) and click a SNP for its evidence card.
  4. Add the selection to your Design panel, compare it with your chip and download vendor files.
  5. No list? Search a gene, rs ID or region, or browse the known loci.

Questions

My positions are on ICSASG_v2

The portal needs Ssal_v3.1. Rows with an rs ID in their id column are moved for you: when a row's position is its rs ID's ICSASG_v2 position in EVA release 9, it gets the rs ID's Ssal_v3.1 position (alleles complemented where the strands differ; the results show position_source). Convert other positions offline with snpscore/prep/lift_icsasg_v2.py in the salmo-snp repository. When more than {{ assembly_share }} of the SNPs checked against the genome have a reference mismatch or look strand-flipped, the upload QC warns that the list is probably on ICSASG_v2 or the other strand.

Which chromosome names work?

1–29, as in Ensembl and AQUA-FAANG, plus the aliases in the release's alias table{% if chrom_aliases %}, e.g. for chromosome 25: {{ chrom_aliases|join:", " }} (any case){% endif %}. The upload QC lists every alias it applied. SNPs on unplaced scaffolds are listed as rejected.

rs IDs and LOC symbols

Search finds rs IDs from EVA release 9 (rs IDs on several Ssal_v3.1 positions are left out) and opens their variant page, which also gives the ICSASG_v2 position. A file of rs IDs can be scored like any list (format). NCBI LOC symbols are found only for genes whose Ensembl description cites the NCBI gene; otherwise use the gene's name, ortholog or position.

Chip manifests

Illumina manifests and Axiom annotation files are read as they are, but their positions are often on ICSASG_v2 and their alleles on the TOP/BOT or probe strand: watch the assembly warning and the ref_check column.

Why don't tiers change when I filter by tissue?

Tiers always count all 21 conditions, so a SNP has the same tier in the Overview, its card, the Design panel and every download. The Table's Chromatin filter only picks rows: SNPs inside a robust ATAC peak (or, if you choose, an active state) in at least one of the conditions you tick.

Where are my results?

The Score page lists the results this browser opened, newest first, until they are deleted ({{ keep_hours }} hours after scoring, or Delete). The list lives in your browser; anyone with a results link can open those results.

Who can see my Design panel?

Only this browser: a cookie names the panel, never a link. It is deleted {{ keep_hours }} hours after you start it; to keep it longer, download its TSV and score that file again later.

What do tiers, evidence terms and chromatin colours mean?

Methods defines the tiers, the evidence weights, the chromatin states with their colours, and a glossary.

Exports and vendor files

Locus names and the lookup file

Vendor files name each SNP Ssa<chromosome>p<position>, e.g. {{ locus_name }} (letters and digits only, unique; chromosome 0 = not on 1–29), because your ids may break vendor naming rules. The lookup file maps each name back to your id, position, alleles, TOP/BOT strand, Illumina A/B alleles, whether it needs two probes (A/T or C/G: Infinium I), assay status and export notes.

Why some SNPs are left out of vendor files

SNPs that fail the assay check, are not checked or have no genome sequence are left out, unless you keep them, flagged, under More formats. A vendor file holds at most {{ vendor_cap|thousands }} SNPs, with flanks from the Ssal_v3.1 genome and alleles on its forward strand. Assay thresholds are provisional: confirm the layout and the designs with your vendor.

Illumina Infinium bins (error 111, warning 604)

A/T SNPs go to Infinium I bin A, C/G SNPs to bin B and the others to Infinium II bin C. A used bin A or B needs at least {{ bins.0 }} SNPs and bin C at least {{ bins.2|thousands }} (error 111); {{ bins.1 }} are recommended in A and B for polyploids such as salmon (warning 604).

SNPs within {{ close_bp }} bp of another (warning 340)

Neighbouring markers can interfere with each other's probes (Illumina warning 340; Thermo Fisher: interfering SNPs can be as close as 10 bases).

Provenance

Every download says which release, rules and data produced it: '#' lines at the top of TSV and BED files, and a provenance file next to vendor files (the zip bundle holds both).

Privacy

Uploaded files are deleted as soon as they have been scored. From a VCF only CHROM, POS, ID, REF and ALT are read, so genotypes are never stored. Other columns of a SNP table, and GWAS summary statistics, are kept with the results for {{ keep_hours }} hours; anyone with the results link can see them. Delete now removes them sooner. The server never logs SNPs or file names. Besides the form-protection cookie, the portal's own cookies remember the results this browser opened (for the Score page), your Design panel and, if you switch chromatin colours, your choice. For confidential lists, run it locally.
