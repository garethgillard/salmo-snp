One toolbar line (the count, the tiers of the rows shown, Clear filters; downloads, Add to Design panel, Columns), a one-line key (the Chromatin cells' states, ~NAME), then the table. Each filter sits on its column: a funnel button in the header (_dd.html), its choice under the column's name; a hidden column has no filter (show it with Columns), and a filtered column always shows. The form #tf holds them all and applies them on change; sort links and pages stay on this page (htmx swaps #results). The Keep column's box takes a row out of the selection or puts it back (a link; x in the card). A click on a row opens its card beside the table (?snp=; over the page below 992 px). Every control is a plain link or GET form, so it all works without JavaScript.{% endcomment %}
